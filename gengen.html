Lesson 1.1: Wages, Salaries, and Compensation Schemes – Definitions

Formal definitions of compensation types, 4 pay periods, payroll process, and GRESMA framework

Compensation
Definition: The money paid to people for their work. It comes in different forms including wages, salaries, and commissions.
Wages
Definition: Earnings based on the number of hours worked or the amount of work completed (common in hourly or factory jobs).
Salaries
Definition: Fixed regular payments, usually given to professionals or office workers who are paid to complete tasks rather than track time.
Commissions
Definition: Earnings based directly on how much a person sells, commonly seen in real estate, insurance, and sales fields.
Payroll
Definition: The business process of tracking employee hours, salaries, deductions, and distributing payment accurately on schedule.
GRESMA Method
Definition: 5-step problem solving framework: Given, Required, Equation, Solution, and Answer.

Lesson 1.2: Overtime (OT) Pay – Article 87 Definitions & Multipliers

Legal definitions and computation formulas under Article 87 of the Philippine Labor Code

Overtime Pay is defined as statutory compensation for work performed beyond 8 regular hours per day, governed by Article 87 of the Philippine Labor Code.

Regular Day Overtime
Definition: Work beyond 8 hours on a regular working day (+25% premium rate). Multiplier: 1.25x.
Rest Day / Special Day Overtime
Definition: Overtime on a scheduled rest day or special non-working day (+30% on 130% base). Multiplier: 1.69x.
Regular Holiday Overtime
Definition: Overtime rendered on an official regular holiday (+30% on 200% base). Multiplier: 2.60x.
Holiday Falling on Rest Day OT
Definition: Overtime on a regular holiday that coincides with a rest day (+30% on 260% base). Multiplier: 3.38x.

Lesson 1.3: Piecework & Commission Schemes – Definitions

Definitions of production incentives, quota thresholds, and sales compensation models

Piecework
Definition: Pay based on the number of items or units finished rather than hours worked. Formula: Gross = Rate Per Item × Quantity.
Differential Piecework
Definition: A piece rate system where workers earn one standard rate up to a set quota, and a higher incentive rate for items produced beyond the quota.
Straight Commission
Definition: A sales compensation scheme where the percentage rate remains constant for all sales volume.
Variable Commission
Definition: A sales compensation scheme where the percentage commission rate increases as sales volume reaches higher milestones.

Lesson 1.4: Philippine Taxes & 12% VAT – Definitions of 11 Tax Types

Official statutory definitions of all taxes collected by the Philippine Government

Taxes are amounts of money collected by the government from individuals and businesses to generate revenue, manage public resources, and fund education, healthcare, and infrastructure.

Excise Tax
Definition: Tax imposed on specific goods such as alcohol, fuel, and tobacco.
Capital Gains Tax (CGT)
Definition: Tax imposed on profit earned from selling assets like land, buildings, or shares.
Documentary Stamp Tax (DST)
Definition: Tax levied on legal documents, promissory notes, and formal financial transactions.
Percentage Tax
Definition: Business tax for small enterprises that are not registered for Value-Added Tax (VAT).
Donor's Tax
Definition: Tax imposed on gratuitous transfers of property or money given as a gift.
Real Property Tax (RPT)
Definition: Annual local ad valorem tax imposed on the ownership of land and buildings.
Business Tax
Definition: Local tax imposed by cities or municipalities for the privilege of operating a business.
Transfer Tax
Definition: Tax imposed when ownership of real property is transferred between parties.
Income Tax
Definition: Tax imposed on the income or net earnings of individuals and businesses.
Value-Added Tax (VAT)
Definition: 12% indirect tax added to the price of goods and services consumed in the Philippines.
Withholding Tax
Definition: Tax deducted in advance by employers from income before releasing salary to workers.

Week 6: Percentages in Business & Finance – Definitions

Definitions of Inflation, Mark-Up, Discount, Cost Price, Selling Price, Profit & Loss

Inflation
Definition: The general increase in prices of goods and services over time, which causes the purchasing power of money to decline.
Mark-Up
Definition: The amount added to the cost price of an item to make a profit: Mark-Up = Selling Price - Cost Price.
Discount
Definition: The amount reduced from the original regular price during a sale: Discount = Original Price - Net Price.
Cost Price (CP)
Definition: The initial amount paid by a business to acquire or manufacture an item.
Selling Price (SP)
Definition: The final price charged to the consumer: Selling Price = Cost Price + Mark-Up.
Net Price
Definition: The actual amount a buyer pays after discount deduction: Net Price = Original - Discount.
Profit (Gain)
Definition: Occurs when total revenue/selling price exceeds total cost (SP > CP): Profit = SP - CP.
Loss
Definition: Occurs when an item is sold for less than what was paid for it (SP < CP): Loss = CP - SP.

Week 7: Patterns in Life and Nature – Definitions

Definitions of patterns, sequences, Fibonacci, fractals, tessellations, and symmetry

Pattern
Definition: A repeated or regular arrangement of shapes, numbers, designs, or events that follow a recognizable, predictable rule.
Sequence
Definition: An ordered list of numbers (terms) that follow a specific mathematical governing rule explaining how each term is obtained.
Fibonacci Sequence
Definition: A sequence where each term is the sum of the two preceding terms (Fn = Fn-1 + Fn-2), starting with 0 and 1.
Fractals
Definition: Infinitely complex, self-similar repeating geometric patterns found across varying scales in nature (trees, ferns, rivers).
Tessellations
Definition: Repeating arrangements of geometric tile shapes that cover a surface completely without gaps or overlaps (honeycombs).
Bilateral Symmetry
Definition: Symmetry where an object is divided along a single central axis into two mirror-image left and right halves (butterflies).
Radial Symmetry
Definition: Symmetry where identical parts radiate outward symmetrically from a central point or axis (starfish, daisies).
Harmonic Sequence
Definition: A progression formed by taking the reciprocals of an arithmetic progression (1, 1/2, 1/3, 1/4...).

Week 8: Arithmetic & Geometric Sequences in Real Life – Definitions

Definitions of linear vs exponential growth models, common differences, and ratio multipliers

Arithmetic Sequence
Definition: A mathematical sequence modeling linear steady growth with fixed constant increments added over equal intervals: an = a1 + (n - 1)d.
Common Difference (d)
Definition: The constant value added to each term in an arithmetic progression: d = an - an-1.
Geometric Sequence
Definition: A mathematical sequence modeling exponential growth or decay through constant multiplication over equal intervals: an = a1 · r^(n - 1).
Common Ratio (r)
Definition: The constant multiplier in a geometric progression: r = an ÷ an-1.
Growth Multiplier
Definition: The common ratio for exponential growth situations, defined as r = 1 + Growth Rate (e.g. 5% growth → r=1.05).
Depreciation Multiplier
Definition: The common ratio for exponential decay and asset depreciation, defined as r = 1 - Depreciation Rate (e.g. 15% depreciation → r=0.85).
Installment Plan
Definition: A structured financial arrangement where a buyer pays fixed periodic increments over time, modeled arithmetically.
Asset Depreciation
Definition: The gradual reduction in monetary value of physical property over time due to wear or obsolescence, modeled geometrically.

Effective Communication – Unit 1: Lessons 3 & 4

High-yield taxonomy: Properties of a Well-Written Text, Modes of Expression, 4 Core Processes & Metacognition

Coherence
Definition: The overall macro-level 'oneness' and logical unity of ideas where all sentences and paragraphs stick together logically.
Cohesion
Definition: Grammatical and lexical linking at the sentence level signaling shared referents across clauses.
Transitional Devices
Definition: Connector words signaling addition (moreover), time (meanwhile), result (consequently), or contrast (on the contrary).
Personal Narrative
Definition: Subjective narrative text structure recounting a specific event at a given place/time based on personal feelings and impressions.
Multimodal Expression
Definition: Combining multiple channels (text, audio, video, graphics) for impactful expression (e.g. vlogging); guided by "Think before you click".
4 Core Processes
Definition: 1. Interpreting (Understanding Meaning) · 2. Engaging (Showing Presence) · 3. Expressing (Sharing with Purpose) · 4. Reflecting (Evaluating Oneself).
Metacognition
Definition: Thinking about one's own thinking — monitoring, evaluating, and adapting communication before, during, and after interactions.
Lucas (2020) Framework
Definition: Self-reflection transforms raw experience into intentional learning, heightening self-awareness and communicative effectiveness.
Reflective Expression
Definition: Storytelling conveys what happened; reflection answers why it matters and gives the audience a lasting reason to remember.
4 Growth Strategies
Definition: 1. Setting Clear Goals · 2. Seeking Feedback · 3. Intentional Practice · 4. Tracking Progress to correct mistakes before they become habits.
